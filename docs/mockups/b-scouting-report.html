Three things to know

  1. Fight him before minute 15.

    Your Stridebreaker is usually done around minute 13, his Eclipse around 15. Those two minutes are the best window in the lane.

  2. Build Sterak's Gage second.

    It adds 2.8 points of win chance over the average second item, the clearest effect of any item choice in this matchup.

  3. Skip Hullbreaker.

    It wins 61% of games, but mostly because players buy it when they are already ahead. Its own effect is −3.6 points.
