Reading the build

The path shows an observed three-item core from real matches, with its most common first component and boots. Those two additions were not necessarily bought together in every game. Each timing is the average among its buyers. Opponent timings use their own most common build. Later items need games that lasted long enough to reach them.

What the model measures

Before a purchase, the model predicts win chance from recorded game state. It then compares buyers' actual wins with that prediction and the slot average. This is an adjusted association, not the true effect of the item. Reasons for choosing it, player skill and later events can still differ.

Split-half checks test stability, not causality. The curve after a purchase tracks the whole game, including fights, objectives and later items. The default path is the most played complete build; alternatives show observed win rates. Matchup win rates use Wilson intervals. Collection is a convenience sample.

About Settistics

What it is

Settistics shows how League of Legends item, rune and summoner spell choices performed in ranked solo queue, per champion, role and lane matchup, with sample sizes and uncertainty next to every number. The goal is to help players understand which choices tend to work and why, not to play the game for them.

Data we use

Ranked solo queue match results and timelines from the official Riot Games API (Match-V5), plus public item, rune and champion data from Data Dragon. Matches are gathered from a sample of ranked players and processed on our side into champion-level totals.

Privacy

The site only publishes aggregated counts per champion, role, opponent, patch and region. It never shows player names, Riot IDs, account identifiers or match IDs, and nothing on the page can be traced back to an individual player. Player identifiers are used internally only to find matches and are not shared. The site sets no cookies, uses no tracking or analytics, and loads nothing from third parties.
